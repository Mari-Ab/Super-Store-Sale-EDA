# Super-Store-Sale-EDA

Exploratory data analysis of Global Superstore sales (2011–2014): finding profit killers with Python & pandas.

## Key Findings

- Discount is the #1 profit killer (−0.85 correlation); break-even at 20–30% discount
- 1 in 4 orders loses money (24.5%) — loss orders average 45% discount vs 4%
- Phones = revenue king ($1.7M); Copiers = profit champion ($450K); Tables = danger zone
- Fast delivery doesn't eat profit — ~$28/order across all ship modes
- Sales doubled 2011–2014 ($2.3M → $4.3M); 2013 was the breakout year
- US dominates (~$2.3M); Canada & EMEA are loss-making markets
